## What changed in the animation overhaul
Old rig: target-point IK, three smoothstepped keys per move, exponential pose smoothing, no secondary motion. Replaced end to end:

- `src/ink/skeleton.js` — side-view skeleton. Channels: pelvis px/py, `lean` (lower spine), `bend` (chest), `head`, `twist`, hands relative to shoulders (fx,fy,bx,by), feet in floor space (ffx,ffy,bfx,bfy), foot pitch, whole-body `rot` about the waist, `stick` (pull a floating body onto the floor). Bone lengths: arms 34+33, legs 50+48, spine 23+34, head 23. Two-bone IK with a fixed bend side (elbows down, knees forward) so limbs cannot fold the wrong way. Floor clamp lifts any body part that would sink under the floor.
- `src/ink/track.js` — monotone cubic Hermite tracks per channel (velocity continuous through keys, no stop-start). Hands interpolate in polar form about the shoulder so arms swing through arcs. Per-channel time lag (`LAG`) makes the kinetic chain: pelvis leads, shoulders follow, hands arrive, head lags. Attack clips are authored in seconds from the moment of contact and stretched onto each move's real timing; lag fades at both ends so a move always starts and ends exactly in the stance.
- `src/ink/clips.js` — every authored motion: 14 attack clips (+ aliases for throws/finishers), hit reactions by region (head/body/leg/recoil), knockdown (`back`, `swept`, `ko`), get-up, backstep, forward roll, thrower and victim clips for the four grapples. Rolled poses author feet "hanging" below the pelvis (`hang()`), because rotation is applied after IK.
- `src/ink/animator.js` — per-fighter state machine: procedural locomotion (idle breathing/bounce/sway, distance-driven shuffle gait with planted feet, squat-style duck, guard hands, air pose from vertical velocity, landing dip), clip playback, crossfades on every category change, and a spring layer (hit/block/parry/recoil/land impulses on lean, bend, head, pelvis, hands). `visualState()` resolves the frame between a hit and the fighter's next tick so poses never glitch. Re-hits on a body that is already falling continue the fall instead of restarting it.
- `src/ink/motion.js` — thin: `strikePose`/`strikePoint`/`strikeChannels` read the same clip the renderer plays, so damage reach follows the visible fist or toe. Also owns `RISE_TIME` (0.62 s) and `DODGE_TIME` (0.46 s).
- `src/ink/rig.js` — silhouette drawing (tapered limbs, torso curve, tunic skirt, foot, head with topknot and headband), verlet cloth (two headband tails, two sash tails) simulated in world space, brush-streak trails behind fast limbs, a quick squeeze when the fighter turns. `sample(f,t)` gives a deterministic spring-free pose for tests and tools; `advance`/`draw` are the stateful path; `reset()` clears everything for a new round.
- `src/ink/sequences.js` — throw and finisher paths are now one function, `trajectory(kind,t)`, used by the simulation to place the victim and by the animator to rotate it (shoulder throw and Falling Moon are a 270° forward flip; reaping throw is a swept backward fall; Silent Bell is an overhead press and slam).
- `src/ink/combat.js` — small additions: `stunTotal`, parry sets the attacker's `reaction='recoil'`, landing records `landPower`, rise takes `RISE_TIME` with 0.3 s of invulnerability.
- `src/ink/stage.js` — forwards hit/block/parry/land events to the rigs, freezes rig motion during hit-stop (`hitstop` flag from main.js), removed the cosmetic spirit arc (trails replace it).
- `src/ink/main.js` — `resetRound()` resets rigs; `?debug` exposes `window.__fury` (stage, fighters, input, audio) for browser QA.

## Controls
A/D or arrows move, S/down duck, Space jump, J fist (three hit-confirm chain), K palm, L kick (chains wheel kick); S+J body blow, S+L sweep, W+J or S+K rising fist; aerial J/L. Shift guard / time for parry; Q backstep or direction+Q roll; E shoulder throw, S+E reaping throw; F Silent Bell, S+F Falling Moon; R spirit (60 meter). Esc pauses. Local P2 arrows move/duck; numpad0 jump, 1/2/3 attacks, 4 dodge, 5 throw, 6 spirit, decimal finisher, right Shift guard. Standard controller: stick move/duck, A jump, X/Y/RB attacks, LB guard, B dodge, LT throw, RT spirit, View finisher, Menu pause. Touch first player; force on in settings for testing. Practice has infinite spirit and selectable still/sparring partner.

## Validation (this session)
- `npm test`: 74/74 (the 2D motion suite was rewritten: reach limits, bend side, constant bone lengths, planted support foot, start/end in stance, no frame-to-frame jumps, speed at contact, reach vs visible fist in both facings, no foot skating while walking, crossfades, spring overshoot/settle, grapple paths, all states finite). Legacy 3D tests still run and pass.
- `node tools/analyze-motion.mjs [name] [--keys]` — reach clamp / floor lift / speed report per clip. Known acceptable items: `upper` 1 unit and `round` 3.7 units of reach drag during recovery overlap; lying poses are lifted by the floor clamp by design.
- `node tools/pop-audit.mjs <seed> <seconds> [--down]` — plays AI vs AI through the real sim and animator and lists the largest single-frame jumps by transition. After the fixes there are no jumps in locomotion; the remaining large ones (40–60 units in a frame) are the first two to three frames of a heavy hit or knockdown, which is the intended snap.
- `node tools/bench.mjs` — roughly 18 µs per fighter per frame for step + solve + paint calls on a stub canvas.
- Visual review was done frame by frame in `tools/motion-lab.html` and through the real stage in `tools/arena-sheet.html?s=<scenario>&from=<tick>&every=<n>&count=<n>`. Scenarios: jab, cross, combo, kick, wheel, palm, upper, sweep, throw, reap, spirit, jump, jumpkick, dodgeback, dodgeroll, walk, retreat, duck, guard, block, parry, hit, hitkick, knockdown, finish, finishlow.
